# Portfolio

Tevin Rivera's personal portfolio site (tevdevelops.com). It presents his work and experience to recruiters and hiring managers for senior frontend and full-stack roles.

## Language

### Work

**Case Study**:
A long write-up of one initiative (problem, role, approach, outcome, with media) that has its own page. It belongs to a Role or is marked Personal, and is either Published or In progress.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, project page, engagement

**Chapter**:
A titled section of a Case Study that covers one thread of the initiative.
_Avoid_: Part, sub-case-study

**Project**:
A short summary (blurb, stack, links, year) of a smaller or older piece of work. It has no page of its own.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, side project (unless it is one)

**Earlier work**:
The list of Projects shown on the home page.
_Avoid_: Archive (until a dedicated Projects page exists)

### Experience

**Role**:
A title held at an organization over a date range. Roles make up the experience timeline, and a promotion within one organization is a separate Role.
_Avoid_: Job, position, experience entry

### Writing

**Post**:
A dated piece of writing published under Writing.
_Avoid_: Blog post, article
